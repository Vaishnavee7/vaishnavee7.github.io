
# Hi, I'm Vaishnavee Thote 👋

### Computational Biologist | Bioinformatics | Machine Learning

📍 Bonn, Germany

I’m a computational biologist who is interested in developing computational methods to identify and understand biological signals. I work with single cell data (mainly scRNA and scATAC) and find ways to integrate them. I am currently expanding my single cell experience by working with CRISPR screens data.

I completed my Master's in **Life Science Informatics at the University of Bonn**, with my Master's thesis at **Helmholtz Munich** focusing on disentangling cellular responses to complex perturbations using single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq data.

My goal is to combine computational biology, machine learning, and statistical modelling to uncover biological mechanisms and generate insights with translational relevance.
